# Aurora Reviewer Portal — User Guide

## Signing In

Sign in with your usual company credentials. You'll land on the **Review Queue**.

## Reviewing a Document

1. Click a document in the queue to open the detail view.
2. Read the document in the inline preview.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Request Changes**.
4. Optionally leave a comment explaining your decision.

## Tracking Status

Your decisions are recorded immediately and visible to the submitter. Use the
**Status** filter on the queue to see documents by state.
