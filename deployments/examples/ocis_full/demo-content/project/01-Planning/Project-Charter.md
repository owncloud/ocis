# Project Charter — Aurora

## Problem Statement

Document reviews currently happen over email with no tracking, no visibility into
status, and no consistent record of who approved what. Reviewers regularly lose
track of pending items, and there is no way to audit past decisions.

## Objectives

1. Provide a self-service review queue for reviewers
2. Automate routing of documents to the correct reviewer
3. Record every decision (approve/reject/comment) with a timestamp

## Scope

**In scope:** internal document review, notifications, audit log
**Out of scope:** external/customer-facing review flows (phase 2)
