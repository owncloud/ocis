# Aurora — Project Overview

**Aurora** is a demo initiative to modernize the internal document review workflow,
replacing a manual, email-based process with a self-service portal built on
oCIS Infinite Scale.

## Goals

- Cut average document turnaround time from 5 days to 1 day
- Give reviewers a single place to see what's pending
- Keep a full audit trail of every review decision
