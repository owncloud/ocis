Enhancement: Update mermaid to 12.3.0

Mermaid has been updated from 11.16.1 to 12.3.0 in the Web UI, and the mermaid
editor app no longer allows older, vulnerable versions. Flowchart, state, class
and entity-relationship diagrams are now laid out with ELK by default, so
existing diagrams may look different. Mermaid 12 also requires Safari 17.4 or
newer.
