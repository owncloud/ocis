Enhancement: Sort "Shared with me" by "Shared by"

The "Shared by" column in "Shared with me" can now be sorted. Items are ordered
by the alphabetised display names of all the people who shared them, joined
into one key, so an item shared by several people is placed consistently
regardless of which avatar happens to display first. Clicking the column
header again reverses the order. The order of the avatars shown in the
column and the sidebar is unaffected.

Sorting by "Shared with" no longer reorders the avatars in that column. They
used to stay sorted alphabetically after switching to another sort, until the
page was reloaded.
